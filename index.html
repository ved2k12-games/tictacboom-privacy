TicTacBoom · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TicTacBoom mobile application (the "App") handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, address or phone number.

The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA)

  • App usage and interaction data with advertisements

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device only.

  • To display advertisements, including personalized ads where you have given consent
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, advertising identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other apps or websites. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking) or via adssettings.google.com.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent through a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personal data is used for personalized advertising. Where consent is not given, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the privacy settings option in the App.

The legal bases for processing are your consent and our legitimate interest in operating and improving the App.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Because we do not directly store personal data, many requests are best handled by the relevant third-party provider (such as Google). You can also contact us using the email below and we will help.

6. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, for the purposes described. Data collected by those providers is retained according to their own policies.

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
